Privacy Policy

Updated: October 2026

This is a personal, non-commercial OAuth application for the account holder to connect Rclone to their own Google Drive for backup and recovery.

Google Drive access

Once authorized, the Rclone software installed on the account holder's systems can access the Drive data permitted by the Google OAuth consent. It is used to store and restore the account holder's backup data. The exact permissions depend on the scopes approved during setup.

Storage and sharing

Rclone stores OAuth credentials locally in its configuration on the account holder's system; they are not published to this website. Backup data resides in the account holder's chosen Google Drive location and is intended to be encrypted before upload. No Google Drive data is sold or provided to third parties by this personal integration.

Website data

These pages contain no login form, analytics script, or tracking code. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request data under GitHub's privacy statement.

Access and revocation

Authorization can be revoked by the account holder in their Google Account connections. The Rclone connection and locally stored credentials can also be removed from their own system.
